## Arabic IR Papers

This repository contains all papers addressing Arabic IR that are referenced in our work.  
It is intended to serve as a resource for research in this domain and will be updated over time to provide a reliable reference.

Each paper is annotated according to the challenges defined in the paper (C1–C7). The challenges referenced throughout correspond to the following categories:

- **C1 — Lack of Public Resources**  
- **C2 — Language Complexity**  
- **C3 — Reproducibility Barriers**  
- **C4 — Domain-Specific Complexity**  
- **C5 — Outdated Public Content**  
- **C6 — Lack of Financial Support**  
- **C7 — Lack of Real-Time Systems**
